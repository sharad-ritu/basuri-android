# UX research for Basuri

Researched 2026-11-05. Scope: improve the native Compose beginner journey, especially the oversized home hero, long instructions, and tuner that displaces fingering.

## Evidence and limits

I inspected the linked first-party images directly. Store images and website illustrations are promotional material, not a live app audit. Several examples show landscape iOS screens. They do not establish Android behavior, accessibility, or a suitable portrait layout. Recommendations below are Basuri-specific design judgments, not measured usability results.

| Product | Observed visuals and documented behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Yousician | The developer's [Piano App Store images](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/piano-by-yousician/id1469033443), images 2 and 4, place notes directly above a highlighted keyboard. A short instruction names the note and finger. Feedback examples distinguish early, correct, and late timing. The listing documents microphone feedback on accuracy and timing. |
| Simply Piano | The developer's [App Store images](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/simply-piano-learn-piano-fast/id1019442026) show a highlighted note at a vertical playhead and named course cards with a play action. The [website's finger-guidance illustration](https://www.hellosimply.com/_next/static/media/pianoPhone03.e0ccd5a5.png) pairs numbered fingers with notation. Its [product page](https://www.hellosimply.com/simply-piano) documents immediate feedback and step-by-step tutorials. |
| Duolingo Music | The [2023 launch article](https://blog.duolingo.com/music-course/) and its [course image](https://storage.ghost.io/c/7a/33/7a33d0f4-927d-4fe8-a6bf-96131b5e76d4/content/images/2023/10/music_hero.png) show a named unit, a portrait path with completed and upcoming nodes, and landscape exercises. ## Six recommendations

1. **Give the next lesson priority on home.** Replace the tall hero with a small bamboo illustration, the configured Sa, and one prominent start or resume action naming the lesson. Put the existing four-step path immediately below, with explicit current and completed labels and repeat access. This adapts Duolingo's named unit and Simply Piano's course selection.

2. **Teach one physical action per step.** Use a short heading and one instruction beside its diagram, such as "Close holes 1, 2, and 3." Follow with hearing the reference, then playing. Put longer posture and breath explanations behind a clearly labeled help action. Yousician's note-and-finger prompt and Simply Piano's numbered-finger illustration support placing guidance at the point of action. Keep numbering from the blowing hole, as specified in [Basuri's music notes](MUSIC_AND_AUDIO.md#fingering-and-relative-sa).

3. **Keep the task, fingering, and feedback together.** In portrait practice, use this order: target swara with configured note and octave, short cue, numbered bamboo fingering, compact horizontal meter, then reference and listening controls. Reduce meter decoration and empty space before reducing diagram or text size. Aim to show the fingering and listening control without scrolling at default text size on a small phone. TonalEnergy supplies the horizontal-meter precedent; Yousician and Duolingo supply the adjacent instrument-guidance precedent.

4. **Make feedback explain the next action.** Keep the target stable while detected pitch changes. Pair meter movement with text such as "Below target," "Above target," or "On target," and show hold progress against the existing match requirement. Distinguish listening without a reliable pitch from an incorrect note. Yousician's specific feedback and TonalEnergy's directional indicator support this approach. Preserve Basuri's [existing pitch rules and limitations](MUSIC_AND_AUDIO.md#audio-processing). A pitch match cannot confirm fingering, breath quality, or rhythm. Keep self-guided completion distinct from microphone-confirmed completion.

5. **Offer recovery where listening fails.** Request microphone access when the learner chooses to listen, with a short explanation of pitch checking. After denial, keep reference playback, diagrams, and self-guided practice available. Android's [permission guidance](https://developer.android.com/training/permissions/requesting#basic-principles) supports contextual requests and continued use after denial. For an intentional retry, offer relevant permission help without repeated prompts. Simply Piano's [microphone help](https://piano-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/12647864-enable-microphone-access-for-simply-piano) distinguishes app permission from the system microphone switch. Separately explain no reliable pitch and capture failure, with one appropriate next action and retry. Do not claim to know why detection failed when the audio engine cannot establish the cause.

6. **Recover space without shrinking interaction.** Retain Basuri's documented 56 dp main actions and keep every interactive target at least 48 dp, following [Compose accessibility guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/accessibility/api-defaults#minimum-touch-target-sizes). Allow text to wrap and the page to scroll at large font sizes. Label swara selections and listening state in text; encode open and closed holes with shape and a readable description. Give the diagram a useful TalkBack summary. Announce important state changes rather than every pitch frame, consistent with [Compose semantics guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/accessibility/semantics#alerts-and-pop-ups) against frequent live-region updates. Competitor images do not demonstrate accessibility compliance.

Retain the bamboo illustration, indigo palette, relative Sa, and six-hole bansuri model from [Basuri's design](DESIGN.md). Let the instrument guide the visual identity. Borrow task clarity and feedback placement; introduce no competitor branding, XP, streaks, invented accuracy scores, or unmeasured musical judgments.
